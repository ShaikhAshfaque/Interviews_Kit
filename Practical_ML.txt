# 100 ML Numerical Interview Questions with Answers

## 1–10: Probability & Statistics

### 1. A dataset contains 1,000 samples, of which 120 belong to class 1. What is the empirical probability of class 1?

**Answer:**

P(Class 1) = Number of Class 1 samples / Total samples

= 120 / 1000

= **0.12 = 12%**

---

### 2. A fraud detection model identifies 90 out of 100 actual fraud cases. What is the recall?

**Answer:**

Recall = TP / (TP + FN)

= 90 / 100

= **0.90 = 90%**

---

### 3. 60% of customers are male and 40% are female. Among males, 10% purchase a product; among females, 20% purchase it. What is the overall probability of purchase?

**Answer:**

P(Purchase) = P(Purchase|Male)P(Male) + P(Purchase|Female)P(Female)

= (0.10 × 0.60) + (0.20 × 0.40)

= 0.06 + 0.08

= **0.14 = 14%**

---

### 4. Given P(A)=0.6, P(B)=0.5, and P(A∩B)=0.3, calculate P(A|B).

**Answer:**

P(A|B) = P(A∩B) / P(B)

= 0.3 / 0.5

= **0.6**

---

### 5. P(Spam)=0.2, P(Not Spam)=0.8, P(Offer|Spam)=0.9, P(Offer|Not Spam)=0.1. Calculate P(Spam|Offer).

**Answer:**

P(Spam|Offer)

= [P(Offer|Spam)P(Spam)] / P(Offer)

P(Offer) = (0.9 × 0.2) + (0.1 × 0.8)

= 0.18 + 0.08

= 0.26

Therefore:

= 0.18 / 0.26

= **0.6923 = 69.23%**

---

### 6. Dataset: 2, 4, 6, 8, 10. Calculate mean, variance and standard deviation.

**Answer:**

Mean:

(2+4+6+8+10)/5 = **6**

Squared deviations:

16, 4, 0, 4, 16

Sum = 40

Population variance:

40/5 = **8**

Standard deviation:

√8 = **2.828**

---

### 7. Calculate population variance for 10, 12, 14, 16, 18.

**Answer:**

Mean = 14

Squared deviations:

16, 4, 0, 4, 16

Sum = 40

Variance = 40/5

= **8**

---

### 8. Calculate sample variance for 5, 7, 9, 11, 13.

**Answer:**

Mean = 9

Squared deviations:

16, 4, 0, 4, 16

Sum = 40

Sample variance = 40/(5-1)

= **10**

---

### 9. A feature has mean 50 and standard deviation 10. Calculate z-score for x=75.

**Answer:**

Z = (x - mean) / standard deviation

= (75 - 50) / 10

= **2.5**

---

### 10. A normally distributed feature has mean 100 and standard deviation 15. Calculate z-score for x=70.

**Answer:**

Z = (70 - 100) / 15

= -30/15

= **-2**

# Quick Formula Sheet

**Mean**

Mean = Σx / n

**Population Variance**

Variance = Σ(x - μ)² / n

**Sample Variance**

Variance = Σ(x - x̄)² / (n-1)

**Standard Deviation**

SD = √Variance

**Z-score**

Z = (x - μ) / σ

**MAE**

MAE = Σ|y - ŷ| / n

**MSE**

MSE = Σ(y - ŷ)² / n

**RMSE**

RMSE = √MSE

**R²**

R² = 1 - SSE/SST

**Sigmoid**

σ(z) = 1/(1+e^-z)

**Precision**

Precision = TP/(TP+FP)

**Recall**

Recall = TP/(TP+FN)

**F1-score**

F1 = 2 × Precision × Recall / (Precision + Recall)

**Specificity**

Specificity = TN/(TN+FP)

**FPR**

FPR = FP/(FP+TN)

**Accuracy**

Accuracy = (TP+TN)/(TP+TN+FP+FN)

**Gini Impurity**

Gini = 1 - Σpᵢ²

**Entropy**

Entropy = -Σpᵢ log₂(pᵢ)

**Information Gain**

IG = Parent Entropy - Weighted Child Entropy

**Euclidean Distance**

d = √Σ(xᵢ-yᵢ)²

**Manhattan Distance**

d = Σ|xᵢ-yᵢ|

**Min-Max Scaling**

x' = (x-min)/(max-min)

**Gradient Descent**

w_new = w - η∇L

**L1 Penalty**

λΣ|w|

**L2 Penalty**

λΣw²
